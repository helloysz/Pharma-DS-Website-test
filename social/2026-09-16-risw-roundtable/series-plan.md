## Post 4 — position and differentiator
Careful post: differentiate without naming or dismissing other communities. Draft angles to pick from:
- **Built by the field, not for an audience** — the agenda comes from the room (posts 1 and 2 are the proof).
- **Statistics-grade rigor applied to AI** — hosted by the New England Statistical Society; regulatory and
  industry statisticians in the same conversation as AI practitioners.
- **Regulatory reality, not demos** — the community sits next to RISW; the work has to survive review.
- **A working community, not a content feed** — workstreams, projects and mentorship between conferences.
Needs from you: which two or three of these you want to own publicly.
