# RISW 2026 follow-up series — 4 posts

One event, four posts, one job: turn a 3.5-hour roundtable into a visible community that people
ask to join. Each post ends by pointing at the next one, so followers have a reason to stay.

| # | Angle | Asset | Suggested date | Primary audience |
|---|-------|-------|----------------|------------------|
| 1 | **The room** — group photo, thank-you, series tease | Poster in this folder | Sept 17 (Thu) | Contributors first, newcomers second |
| 2 | **What the room said** — survey results | 3–5 result cards (same poster system) | Sept 23–25 | Practitioners: "these are my questions too" |
| 3 | **Where we're going** — PharmaDS 2027 theme & topics | Theme reveal poster + save-the-date | Oct 1–8 | Speakers, sponsors, program committee |
| 4 | **Why us** — position & differentiators vs. other pharma AI communities | Positioning graphic | Oct 13–15 | Anyone deciding where to spend their time |

Cadence: one post a week, Tue–Thu, 8–10am ET. Close enough to keep momentum, far enough apart that
each post gets its own audience.

## Post 2 — survey results
Lead with the single most surprising number, not a summary. Structure: the question → the split →
what it means for the 2027 program. Two or three charts maximum, same brand palette as the poster
(cyan / indigo / amber). Explicitly credit the room: "this came from the people in last week's photo."
Needs from you: the raw survey questions + counts.

## Post 3 — 2027 theme and topics
Frame it as *earned*, not decided: the theme came out of the roundtable, the survey shaped the
topics. Include the call to action that matters most at that point — call for presentations,
committee/volunteer signup, save the date. Needs from you: confirmed theme wording, dates, venue.

## Post 4 — position and differentiator
Careful post: differentiate without naming or dismissing other communities. Draft angles to pick from:
- **Built by the field, not for an audience** — the agenda comes from the room (posts 1 and 2 are the proof).
- **Statistics-grade rigor applied to AI** — hosted by the New England Statistical Society; regulatory and
  industry statisticians in the same conversation as AI practitioners.
- **Regulatory reality, not demos** — the community sits next to RISW; the work has to survive review.
- **A working community, not a content feed** — workstreams, projects and mentorship between conferences.
Needs from you: which two or three of these you want to own publicly.
